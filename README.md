
# TravelFlow

## What It Does
- TravelFlow is a browser‑based travel planner that helps you turn a destination idea into a structured trip. It guides you through setting your location and dates, viewing helpful insights like weather, packing notes, and budget breakdowns, and building a clear day‑by‑day itinerary with transportation and nearby places. Everything saves automatically in your browser, making it simple to plan and adjust your trip without needing an account.

## How To Use It
- You can use TravelFlow directly in your browser by visiting your live site: TravelFlow. From the home page, start a new trip, enter your destination, explore the insights generated for you, add days and activities to your itinerary, and export a clean version to share with anyone joining you.
